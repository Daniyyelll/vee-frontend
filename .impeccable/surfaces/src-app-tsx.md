# Storefront homepage

Mode: Persuade, with clear collection exploration. Audience: cosmetics shoppers;
market remains undecided. Primary action: explore the collection. Approved:
botanical atelier, selected on the decision page; updated by explicit user
steering to exclude all greens. Code-led build. Catalog items are now supplied by the API; hero/ritual art and the bag remain preview concepts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sunlit natural atelier makes the first encounter with Vee feel rich,
comforting, and unhurried, while keeping shopping immediately available.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory, limestone, peach-blush, cocoa, and rose gold from the user reference. Upright sculptural
serif display lettering, quiet sans-serif controls, broad unboxed photographic
surfaces, fine seams, and generous but deliberate spacing. No green.

STORY: A visitor discovers luxury cosmetics through a moment of calm, browses
the live collection, manages their account, reads customer reviews, and previews a personal selection before checkout integration.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A quiet brand navigation above a two-part composition: large
left-aligned display headline on warm ivory and a taller right still life of
ivory and amber cosmetic objects, limestone, and dry branches. Collection action
below the headline. Signature interaction: the still-life softly settles into
place once; the collection filter and preview selection update clearly.

FORM: Botanical atelier, grounded pick one; seed 11a80841. Natural materials
translate the selected couture studio composition. No greens is a pinned rule.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Connected: accounts, profiles, products, categories, uploaded product images, prices, currency, and reviews. Operate mode applies to account and review forms inside the existing native dialog.
Unresolved: checkout, market, payments, shipping, persistent sessions, and deployment. No ingredient, medical, environmental, or manufacturing assertions.
