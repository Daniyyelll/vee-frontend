# First storefront direction research

Working scope: a storefront homepage for cosmetics shoppers. Market and brand
references remain pending. Vee's differentiating product mechanism is not yet
established; the page must present the collection honestly and help shoppers
find a product. The cultural home is beauty counters, personal cosmetic rituals,
fashion photography, and well-made collectible packaging.

The predictable category page is a neutral campaign hero above product cards;
its predictable opposite is an aggressively typographic monochrome editorial.
Neither defines the grounded candidate list.

Grounded directions, ordered by working audience resonance:

1. Couture color room: fabric color fields and a studio still life.
2. Perfumery display cabinet: product niches with clear object labels.
3. Fashion contact sheet: a sequence of considered beauty images and selections.
4. Lacquer compact: a precise outer shell revealing a pearl collection interior.
5. Beauty ritual notebook: useful product discovery through an orderly ritual index.
6. Mirrored salon: paired composition and deliberate reflection.
7. Professional shade fan: color-led browsing with accurate comparison surfaces.

Seed 11a80841 assigned candidate four. The six challengers were fused with
cosmetic shopping and judged on audience identification and product clarity.
The amber light hall is competitive on identification but loses shade clarity;
the remaining challengers lose both axes under the current brief. Their system
discipline raises are recorded on the assigned card, without borrowing motifs.

The user's pinned brief and pending answers take precedence over this research.
